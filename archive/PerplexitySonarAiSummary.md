# AI Tools & Workflows

## Replet Design Mode & Gemini 3
- **How it's used:**  
  1. Work with AI (e.g., Claude, Gemini 3) to generate detailed prompts describing the website or app, including design elements like full screen videos, animations, or specific aesthetics.  
  2. Paste the prompt into Replet design mode, switching from build to design mode for front-end generation.  
  3. Edit or remove placeholders (e.g., video placeholders) and instruct AI to generate AI videos or animations as backgrounds or interactive elements.  
  4. Review and refine output through back-and-forth prompt adjustments.  
  5. Publish with a single click; the site/app goes live immediately.  
- **Key tips:**  
  - Use detailed, specific descriptions in prompts to get the desired design (e.g., specifying full screen videos for each country screen).  
  - Break down ambitious projects into milestones to manage scope effectively (e.g., for personal websites).  
  - Utilize Replet's mobile app for image pasting and prompt editing on the go.  
  - For functional features (beyond front-end), switch to build mode to add back-end logic and databases.

# Product Management Techniques

- **Scope management through milestone breakdowns:** When working with AI-generated specs, breaking large projects into smaller, manageable phases helps avoid overambitious outcomes and ensures iterative progress.  
- **Iterative prompt refinement:** Product managers should expect and plan for multiple rounds of prompt adjustments to align AI outputs with product goals.  
- **Rapid prototyping for stakeholder feedback:** Using AI-generated front-end designs (e.g., AI agent builder interface) as clickable or visual demos can validate ideas with customers or teams before investing in full development.  
- **Single-click publishing:** Emphasize quick deployment to gather real user feedback and iterate faster.
